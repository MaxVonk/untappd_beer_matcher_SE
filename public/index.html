🔑 How to get your session cookie in 3 simple steps:
1
Log in to Untappd

Open untappd.com in your regular browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) and make sure you are signed in.

2
Open Developer Tools (F12)

Press F12 (or right-click ➔ Inspect), switch to the Network tab, and refresh the page (F5).

3
Copy the Cookie Header

Click the top request (e.g. home), scroll to Request Headers, right-click Cookie: and select Copy value.

💡 Alternative: Use a browser extension like Cookie-Editor to export the cookie in 1 click.
